Folio Fracas

A goofy storybook party brawler. Up to 4 players (8 possible), bots fill the gaps.

Keyboard or gamepad. Last one standing wins.

Play

Pick your fighters

Move: WASD / arrows. Jump: W / Space. Light: J. Heavy (hold, release): K. Special: L (Down+L for alt). Shield/dodge: Shift. Taunt: T. Drop through pages: Down. Gamepad: A jump, X light, Y heavy, B special, bumpers shield.
